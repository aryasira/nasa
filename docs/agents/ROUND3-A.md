## 1. IM-1 headline cause: settle it (live-review item 4, the top priority)

The IM-1 cause line is always "nearby terrain, < 0.5 km, az 69–70°". That is one or two cells of the 80 m grid, and IM-1 is our lead example.

Test whether that blocker is real:
- **Resolution:** rebuild the IM-1 horizon from the finest LOLA/PGDA south-pole product you can get (the 20 m or 40 m polar DEM if one is available; say which one you used). Compare the elevation and distance profile at az 60–80° against the 80 m version.
- **Position:** rebuild with the site moved ±1 grid cell (and by the published landing-position uncertainty, if one exists) in 4 directions. Does the blocker stay?
- **Mast height:** rerun at 1 m and 3 m as well as 2 m.

Write the results to `pipeline/reports/im1_near_field.json` and a short section in `docs/science.md`.

**Decision rule:**
- If the blocker survives all of the above, it stays, and the page may call it "nearby terrain".
- If it does not, tell Agent C. C will change the IM-1 cause wording to "nearby terrain, low confidence", or drop that line.

In both cases the main IM-1 story (155 → 82 h, lunar day/night) does not depend on this blocker. Confirm that in the report.
